FAQ

How long does a build take?

A landing page takes 3–5 days. A multi-page site or a store takes 1–2 weeks, depending on scope.

How does payment work?

30–50% before work starts, the remainder once the finished site is delivered.

Can I request changes along the way?

Yes — revisions during the build are included at no extra cost. Changes after handover are agreed separately.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. You get clean code with no page-builder lock-in, so you can edit it yourself, pass it to another developer, or come back to me.

Do you work with clients outside Ukraine?

Yes. I work with clients worldwide, in English, over Telegram or email. Time zones have not been a problem so far — I reply within the day.
